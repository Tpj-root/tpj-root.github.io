--------------------
Daily Repeat Work Routine (No Exhaustion)
--------------------

Start with a micro ritual
Do one small action before you start work every day:
Drink a glass of water
2–3 slow breaths
Sit straight for 10 seconds 
This gives your brain a start signal → reduces resistance.

--------------------
Use “50–10” or “25–5” Pattern

Don’t work continuously. 

Work in cycles:

Work           ----   Break
45–50 min  ----  10 min break
or 25 min     ----    5 min break

Break = stretch body, walk, water, 
DON’T use social media deeply. 
This prevents mental tiredness.

--------------------
Do the Same Work at the Same Time
Repeat the work at one fixed time every day. 
Your brain forms a habit loop. 
After 14–28 days it becomes automatic → less effort.

--------------------

Use a “Mini Goal” for Each Day
Instead of thinking “I must finish everything”, set a small target:
Today I will complete: 
* 10 pages 
* 30 tasks 
* 2 hours only
Small success = motivation + energy.

--------------------

Slight Variation, Same Work
To avoid boredom:
Change sitting position
Change place once a week
Use different pen/music/arrangement Small changes keep the work fresh.

--------------------

Energy Rules
To avoid tiredness, follow these:
Drink water every 2–3 hours
Eat light lunch (avoid too heavy rice/fried foods)
Sleep minimum 6.5–7.5 hours
Stretch body every 2–3 hours

--------------------

Don’t Think Before Starting
Just start for 5 minutes, then continue naturally. This is called the 5-minute rule.
“Start first. Motivation comes later.”

--------------------

Bonus Mindset: Focus on Process, Not Feelings
Don’t ask:
“Am I feeling motivated?”
Instead ask:
“What is the next step?”
Emotion doesn’t control you. Habit controls you.

--------------------

After 30 Days
You will:
Work without stress
Get less tired
Feel automatic
Become more consistent than 90% of people
