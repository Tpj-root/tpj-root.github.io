Hello! I'm Sabari.
I build with C++, image processing, bots, and custom tools.
I’m also deeply interested in collecting old and unused electronic parts.
Every broken board or forgotten chip teaches something —
and many of them can still be revived or transformed.

From discarded circuits to new ideas,
I explore how far a small piece of hardware can go.


This site is my space to share what I build.
